## Adding one

Write a guide when a task took you more than one try. That second try is the whole value —
it means the naive path has a trap in it, and you just learned where.

Rules that keep them useful:

- **Exact commands**, not descriptions of commands.
- **Say what "worked" looks like** after each step, so a reader knows whether to continue.
- **Name the traps** where they happen, not in a troubleshooting section at the bottom.
- **Date it.** Tooling changes; a reader deserves to know they're reading something from 2024.
